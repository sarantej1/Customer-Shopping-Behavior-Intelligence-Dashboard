# Customer-Shopping-Behavior-Intelligence-Dashboard
Interactive Power BI dashboard analysing 99,457 shopping transactions across 10 Istanbul malls — revealing deep patterns in age, gender, category preference, and payment behaviour.

🛍️
99,457 transactions · 10 malls · 8 product categories · 3 payment methods
👩
Female shoppers led at ~60K vs Male ~40K; Clothing dominated all categories
🏪
Mall of Istanbul & Kanyon were top revenue-generating malls
💳
Cash was the highest payment channel by total sales volume
